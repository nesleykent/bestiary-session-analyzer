Bestiary Session Analyzer

This tool analyses your hunting session log to extract monster kill data, calculate your kill rate, and determine the number of remaining kills required to complete a creature's Bestiary entry. It estimates the time needed to reach the final stage by using your current kill speed. This allows you to evaluate and improve your hunting efficiency for Bestiary completion.

Disclaimer

Bestiary Session Analyzer is a community-maintained tool for estimating Tibia Bestiary progress from pasted hunting session logs. Results are derived from the log data you provide and normalized creature metadata from this repository. Estimates reflect observed session kill rates and may vary due to missing log lines, party changes, boosts, prey, rapid respawn events, route differences, server conditions, or normal in-game variation.

Sources and acknowledgments include Tibia.com, CipSoft GmbH, Tibia Fandom for creature reference links, and community guide creators referenced by the dataset. Documentation, source code, and issue reporting are available on GitHub. Want to correct data or report a problem? Use GitHub Issues.
